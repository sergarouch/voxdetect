Anonymous catalog

Lend a few seconds of sound.

This page collects clips for a community voice detector. Silence, noise, English, and other languages are stored as different labels, so they do not get mixed together later.

  • No account, no email, and no name field.
  • Your original filename is thrown away. Clips are numbered, not named.
  • Nothing you send is played back to the next visitor.

How a clip is labeled

Pick the one row that matches what is actually in the recording. The label is chosen here, not typed later.

  1. 01

    Silence

    Nothing you would call a sound. No language is stored.

  2. 02

    Noise

    Sound, but nobody is speaking. You also pick a noise kind: room, traffic, music, typing, crowd, appliance, outdoors, or other.

  3. 03

    English

    A person speaking English. The language is stored as en.

  4. 04

    Other language

    Speech that is not English. You name the language with its code, such as es or zh. If you do not know the code, choose “not sure” and it is stored as und, still separate from English.
